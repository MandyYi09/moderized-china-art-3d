THE ORIGINAL CONCEPT

From pigment
to presence.

The reference paintings offered a visual language: jade clouds, cinnabar armor, lapis outlines, and ribbons suspended in motion.

This new guardian brings those elements into an imagined sculptural world. The concept artwork was created first; the explorable sculpture is a handcrafted procedural interpretation, with its own geometry, proportions, and details.

ABOUT THE WORK

A threshold
between worlds.

The Keeper of the Cloud Gate is an original fantasy guardian inspired by the supplied traditional Chinese guardian paintings. It does not represent a specific historical deity.

A moon-tipped staff marks the boundary of the celestial realm. An open hand carries a pearl of light. Around the figure, ribbons trace the wind, clouds gather, and a lotus anchors the suspended world.

The sculpture is modeled entirely from three-dimensional surfaces: layered armor, individually shaped facial features, openwork crown blades, raised spirals, petal forms, and curved ribbons. Orbit behind it to discover the reverse medallion and hanging cords.
